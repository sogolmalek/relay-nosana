What this page shows, in two minutes

A marketplace pays its sellers every night, and an AI prepares the list of payments. This demo shows a way to stop a wrong payment before it goes out: every AI decision about a large payout comes with a receipt that anyone can check, and the money moves only if the receipt checks out.

The story

  1. Tonight's payout run arrives. Some payouts in it are wrong: a price that changed last week, a seller on a fraud hold, an invoice that was already paid.
  2. The team has one rule: a payout over $500 needs proof.
  3. For those payouts, Relay runs the check on an open AI model on Nosana, a network of GPUs. Nosana keeps a public record of each job: what ran is stored on IPFS, and which machine ran it and when is on the Solana blockchain.
  4. Relay writes one receipt per payout. The receipt points at that public record.
  5. A gate pays a payout only when its receipt checks out and the decision is "approve". Everything else is held or sent to a person.

Why it matters

An ordinary AI service leaves no public record of what it ran, so you have to take its word. Here you don't have to trust Relay, the AI or this page. Your own browser fetches the records from Solana and IPFS and does the checking.

What is real on this page

  • Real: the Nosana jobs, the Solana transactions, the receipts, and every check your browser runs.
  • Test money: the payments are on Solana's test network (devnet).
  • Sample data: the sellers, payouts and team decisions are made up.

Take the tour

  1. Read tonight's payouts and the team's rules.
  2. See which payouts need proof and which don't.
  3. Follow the job from posting to result. Every link opens the public record.
  4. Press "Verify every receipt in your browser". This is the main event.
  5. Open one receipt, watch the six checks, then try to forge it.
  6. See what the gate won't decide alone, and why.
  7. See which payouts were paid, each tied to its receipt.
What you'll see in the first two runs. The small open model that was tested made too many mistakes. So Relay did not let it approve any payout over $500, and those all went to a person. Four small payouts were paid, in the first run. That is the system doing its job: it measured the model and kept it away from the money.
Words used on this page
Receipt
A small file that says what was decided for one payout and where the public record of that work is.
Anchored
The receipt points at a Nosana job on Solana and IPFS, so anyone can check it.
Declared
The receipt only repeats what an AI provider says it ran. There is no public record to check it against.
Node
One computer with a GPU on the Nosana network.
Re-run
The same work run again on a second node, to see whether the answers agree.
The bar
How accurate a model must be, measured against known answers, before it may decide on payouts.
Gate
The last step before money moves. It pays, holds, sends to a person, or refuses.
Tamper
To change a receipt after the fact, as a forger would.

One limit, stated plainly: a receipt proves which job ran, on which node, and when. It does not prove the node computed honestly. Re-runs on a second node cover that.
